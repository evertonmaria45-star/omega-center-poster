Privacy Policy

Omega Center Poster — TikTok for Developers app by OMEGA CENTER (audiovisual agency)
Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. What this app does

Omega Center Poster is a video publishing tool by OMEGA CENTER. It publishes videos to TikTok accounts whose holders authorize it through TikTok Login Kit and reads statistics (views, likes, comments and video count) from those same accounts.

OMEGA CENTER uses the tool for its own accounts and for its clients — creators and churches that hire OMEGA CENTER to produce and publish their videos. Nothing is published on an account without the authorization of the person who holds that account.

2. What data is used

  • Data from the TikTok accounts that authorized the app (username, profile picture, follower count, video count).
  • Videos sent for publication on the authorized accounts.
  • Public statistics of the videos published on those accounts (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • TikTok access tokens (technical authorization credentials), stored only on the computer that runs the tool.

3. How the data is used

Only to: (a) publish the videos sent for publication on the account that authorized the app; (b) show the statistics of those accounts to their holders. No data is used for advertising, third-party profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Data only travels between the authorized TikTok accounts and the tool, through TikTok's official APIs (Login Kit and Content Posting API).

5. Storage and security

Data stays on the computer that runs the tool (local files). TikTok tokens are protected by the operating system's credential store. The tool runs no data server of its own.

6. Your rights

Any account holder can revoke the app's authorization in TikTok settings at any time and can ask OMEGA CENTER to delete the data related to their account. To do so, just contact the person responsible for the app (contact below).

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page.
