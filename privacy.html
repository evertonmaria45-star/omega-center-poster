Privacy Policy

Omega Center Poster — a TikTok for Developers app by OMEGA CENTER
Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. What this app does

Omega Center Poster is an internal OMEGA CENTER tool. It publishes videos only to the TikTok accounts of the app's own owner and reads statistics (views, likes, comments, and video count) from those same accounts.

The app is not open to the public, does not post to third-party accounts, and does not collect data from other people.

2. What data is used

  • Data from the owner's own TikTok accounts (username, profile picture, follower count, video count).
  • Videos the owner uploads for publication on their own accounts.
  • Public statistics of videos on the owner's accounts (views, likes, comments, shares).
  • TikTok access tokens (technical authorization credentials), stored only on the owner's computer.

3. How the data is used

Only to: (a) publish the owner's videos on the owner's accounts; (b) show the statistics of the owner's accounts on the internal dashboard. No data is used for advertising, third-party profiling, or any other purpose.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Data only travels between the owner's accounts and TikTok, through the official API (Content Posting API and Login Kit).

5. Storage and security

Data stays on the owner's own computer (local files). TikTok tokens are protected by the operating system's credential store. The app runs no data server of its own.

6. Your rights

Because this is owner-only use of the owner's own accounts, the owner can revoke the app's authorization in TikTok settings at any time, or request deletion of the local files. Just contact the person responsible for the app (contact below).

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page.
