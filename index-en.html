Ω Omega Center Poster

An internal OMEGA CENTER tool for publishing videos to the owner's own TikTok accounts and reading their statistics.

What this app is

Omega Center Poster is an official TikTok for Developers app created by OMEGA CENTER (audiovisual). It is for internal use by the owner only:

  • Publishes videos only to the owner's own TikTok accounts. It never posts to third-party accounts.
  • Reads statistics (views, likes, comments, and video count) from the owner's own accounts.

This app is not open to the public, does not post to other people's accounts, does not collect third-party data, and does not sell any data.

How it works

  • The owner authorizes the app on their own TikTok accounts (Login Kit).
  • Uploaded videos go only to the accounts authorized by the owner.
  • Statistics are read from those same owner accounts, via the official API (Content Posting API).
