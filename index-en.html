What this app is

Omega Center Poster is an official TikTok for Developers app from OMEGA CENTER (audiovisual agency). It publishes videos to the TikTok accounts that authorize it:

  • Publishes videos to the TikTok accounts whose holders authorize the app through Login Kit — OMEGA CENTER's own accounts and its clients' accounts (creators and churches that hire OMEGA CENTER).
  • Reads statistics (views, likes, comments and video count) from the accounts that authorized the app.

Nothing is published on an account without the authorization of the person who holds it, and that authorization can be revoked in TikTok settings at any time. The app does not sell any data and does not collect data from people who did not authorize it.

How it works

  • The account holder authorizes the app on their own TikTok account (Login Kit).
  • The videos sent go only to the account that authorized the app.
  • The account holder chooses the visibility, and statistics are read through the official API (Content Posting API).
